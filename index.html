Your Rights Under Republic Acts

RA 10627: Anti-Bullying Act of 2013

Mandates non-punitive guidance counseling, strict confidentiality of victim and reporter identities, protection against retaliation, and immediate 24-48 hour institutional response.

RA 10173: Data Privacy Act of 2012

Guarantees the principle of Data Minimization. You have the statutory right to report campus hazards and grievances without being forced to reveal personal identifiable information (PII).
